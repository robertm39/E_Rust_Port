% Rust accepts first-order FOOL term branches here. The C 3.3.5 reference's
% first-order ParseIte path asks TFormulaTSTPParse to parse each branch and
% rejects the comma after the individual-valued `a` branch.
tff(a_type, type, a: $i).
tff(b_type, type, b: $i).
tff(c_type, type, c: $i).
tff(p_type, type, p: $i > $o).
fof(source, axiom, $ite(p(a),a,b) = c).
